Hands-Free Daily Phone Automation

Pair the Blank Dynamic HUD Base above with native iOS Shortcuts or Samsung Good Lock.

iPhone (iOS Shortcuts @ 12:01 AM)

  1. Save ViceCity-Blank-HUD-Base.jpg into an album named ViceHUD.
  2. In Shortcuts, calculate Get Time Between Current Date and Target Date in Days, then Round Up.
  3. Use Find Photos (Album is ViceHUD) → Overlay Text centered inside the neon box → Set Wallpaper Photo (Preview Off).
  4. Trigger daily at 12:01 AM under the Automation tab.

Samsung Galaxy (LockStar + KWGT)

  1. Apply ViceCity-Blank-HUD-Base.jpg as your One UI Lock Screen wallpaper.
  2. Open Good Lock → LockStar and place a transparent 4×2 KWGT widget inside the neon HUD frame (above the fingerprint sensor).
  3. Use KWGT formula: $tf(2026y11M26d0h0m, D)$ DAYS in bold white with a cyan outer glow.
